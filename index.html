WatchTrack

Personal Media Recommendation Tracker

Keep every movie and show recommendation in one place.

WatchTrack helps movie and television viewers save recommendations from friends, family, classmates, and online communities without being limited to one streaming service.

  • Save who recommended each title and why
  • Track viewing progress and personal ratings
  • Search, filter, and sort saved titles
  • Maintain a separate watchlist for each account
